How to use this sheet Print on regular Letter paper (portrait). You get two identical cards per page — cut along the dashed line and fold or stand them on the tables. Print as many pages as you need tents. The QR is vector, so it stays sharp at any size. Check "Background graphics" in your print dialog so the green and gold show up.
