Fold-over table tents — one sheet makes one tent Print on Letter paper, portrait. Print 30 copies for 30 tents. Fold each sheet in half across the dashed line and stand it up — the top half is printed upside down on purpose, so once folded both sides read the right way up. Tick “Background graphics” in the print dialog so the green shows. Card stock stands better than copy paper.
